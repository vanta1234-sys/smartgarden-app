## 5. Houseplant styling shift: fewer, bolder statement plants + water propagation displays

**Trend:** 2026 houseplant-trend coverage (UK design press, horticultural
societies) converges on a shift away from large mixed collections toward a few
large "statement" plants (monstera, dragon trees/ficus varieties), soil-free water
propagation as a decorative display method in its own right (not just a
propagation step), and bolder/more decorative pots.

**Evidence:** [Houseplant trends 2026 – Ideal Home](https://www.idealhome.co.uk/garden/houseplants/houseplant-trends-2026); [5 indoor plant trends worth jumping on in 2026 – Homes & Gardens](https://www.homesandgardens.com/gardens/houseplants-trends-2026).

**Existing content:** "Πολλαπλασιασμός Φυτών με Μοσχεύματα: Ο Πλήρης Οδηγός" and
