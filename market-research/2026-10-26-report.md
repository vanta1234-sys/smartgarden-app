## 3. Native & pollinator-friendly planting, no-till soil health

**Trend:** Multiple 2026 trend roundups converge on the same shift: no-till beds
(compost layered on top instead of dug in), composting treated as routine rather than
optional, and native/pollinator-friendly perennials planted for both biodiversity and
soil structure (deep native roots improving water infiltration and organic matter).

**Evidence:** [The gardening trends for 2026 that prioritize soil health, pollinators, and sustainability – Yahoo Creators](https://creators.yahoo.com/lifestyle/story/the-gardening-trends-for-2026-that-prioritize-soil-health-pollinators-and-sustainability-214426277.html); [Native Plants and Pollinators context – Stanford Sustainable](https://sustainable.stanford.edu/2025/07/11/native-pollinator-plants-make-the-water-wise-garden-more-insect-friendly). One aggregator site repeated specific numeric claims (a "2024 Rutgers study", a UC Davis infiltration figure) that could not be traced to a primary source — excluded from this report.

existing Bokashi composting article and the organic-fertilizing guide, and opens an
affiliate angle on native/pollinator wildflower seed mixes and bee-hotel/insect-house
products (a small but real Greek garden-center SKU category).

**Existing content:** `bokashi-composting-vermicomposting-balcony-20260909` and
