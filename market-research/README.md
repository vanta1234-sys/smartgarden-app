# SmartGarden Market Research

Automated, scheduled research into what's currently trending/selling in the
gardening / smart-garden / balcony-garden space, plus realistic, legitimate
monetization angles for smartgarden.gr (affiliate links, content tie-ins,
product roundup articles). Runs via a scheduled cloud agent.

**Scope boundary:** this looks for *ideas and legitimate affiliate angles*,
not a copycat resale operation. It will never recommend copying another
seller's product designs 1:1 for resale — that's a real IP/trademark risk,
not a growth hack.

Reports land here as dated markdown files: `YYYY-MM-DD-report.md`.
